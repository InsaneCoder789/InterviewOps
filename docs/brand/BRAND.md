# InterviewOps brand

**Name:** InterviewOps (one word, capital I and O)
**Tagline:** Mock interviews, booked without collisions.
**One line:** A Django backend for scheduling mock interviews, where the database itself rejects double-booking.
**Repository:** https://github.com/InsaneCoder789/InterviewOps

## Voice
Plain, exact, engineering-led. Show mechanisms, not adjectives.
Never invent users, testimonials, uptime, performance numbers or a live demo.

## Mark
Two time blocks on adjacent tracks that meet end to start without overlapping:
the half-open `[start, end)` interval the schema enforces.
Files: `docs/brand/logo.svg` (mark and wordmark, readable on light and dark),
`docs/brand/mark.svg` (square mark).
